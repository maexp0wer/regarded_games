Welcome! This is the community forum for Regarded Games, which runs Class War: The Game — class war fought as a perfect-information strategy game with real-money stakes on Base.

This is a place for players to discuss strategy, coordinate with their faction, follow governance votes, and get help. A few things to get you started:

## The basics

- **Be civil.** Treat others as you'd want to be treated. Debate ideas, not people.
- **Search before posting.** Someone may have already asked your question — the 🔍 in the top bar searches every topic.
- **Use the right category.** Posting in the right place helps the right people find your topic.
- **Flag, don't fight.** See something that breaks the rules? Use the flag instead of replying.

## Getting around

- Tap your avatar (top right) to see notifications, messages, and your preferences.
- The hamburger menu (top right) lists categories, tags, and your bookmarks.
- Reply to a topic with the **Reply** button at the bottom; quote someone by selecting their text and choosing **Quote**.

## Your account

You signed in with your wallet, so your username is your address and your faction is set automatically from your live FIM balance. Head to your preferences to set a profile picture and tune your notifications.

Have fun, play sharp, and welcome to the game. 🫡
