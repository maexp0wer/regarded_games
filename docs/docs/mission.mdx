# Mission

## Moving Beyond Speculation

This project begins with a fundamental question: Why does the decentralized world, which promised equality, look so much like the old financial system? Our goal is to confront this reality and build something better.

### A New Framework for Collaboration

Regarded Games is a **live, transparent laboratory** built to test the true power of collective action. We use real money and an open trading environment to directly challenge the old belief that markets must only benefit the few.

You are not just competing; you are taking part in a **grand experiment** to prove that decentralized technology can design systems that genuinely **work for everyone**, not just the wealthy or the insiders.

### The Foundational Critique: Building Systems for the Many

We built this game because we recognized that market rules naturally push wealth toward concentration. Our protocol is engineered to reverse that force.

Our mechanisms—the **Gini Coefficient** as our objective umpire and the **GINI Collateral Rule** as the cost of entry—create a level playing field where the usual solo strategies fail.

By tying your individual reward to the overall state of the community, we steer the strategic focus away from simple personal speculation. This design ensures that **cooperation** becomes the highest-value strategy, proving that systemic fairness and the stability of the whole are the only reliable paths to individual success.
