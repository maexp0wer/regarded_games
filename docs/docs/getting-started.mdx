# Getting Started

This guide takes you from a fresh wallet to your first trade on the seasonal exchange. If you have not yet read the [Intro](/intro), start there to understand the factions and the goal of a season.

## 1. Connect a Wallet

Regarded Games runs on **Base**. You will need an EVM wallet (such as a browser wallet) connected to the correct network:

*   **Mainnet:** Base
*   **Testnet:** Base Sepolia

Open the [App](http://localhost:3000) and connect your wallet. Your faction is not chosen — it is **derived** from your FIM balance relative to the live 50th-percentile threshold.

## 2. Acquire FIM

**FIM** is the in-game token you trade throughout a season. You acquire it directly from other players through the on-chain exchange — there are no hidden pools and no privileged sellers. Every bid, ask, and counterparty is visible on-chain.

Your standing is dynamic:

*   Hold FIM **above** the live threshold and you are a **Capitalist**.
*   Hold FIM **below** it and you are a **Socialist**.

Because the threshold moves with every trade, your faction can shift as the market does.

## 3. Place Your First Trade

Trades are peer-to-peer against a fully transparent order book.

1.  Open the trading terminal in the [App](http://localhost:3000).
2.  Review the live order book — every open bid and ask is on-chain.
3.  Submit a bid or an ask. Approve the transaction, then execute it.
4.  Watch the [Gini Coefficient](/intro#gini-coefficient) respond. Every trade nudges the game's economic state toward concentration or redistribution.

## 4. Play the Season

Each trade is a political act. Capitalists concentrate wealth and push the Gini Coefficient toward **1.0**; Socialists redistribute it toward **0.0**. The winning faction at the end of the season dictates how the USDC prize pool is paid out.

For the full mechanics, economics, and technical design, read the [Whitepaper](/whitepaper).
