# Intro

## 1. Economic Warfare On-Chain

**Regarded Games** is a perfect-information strategy game where **Capital** battles **Coordination** for real-money stakes.

Unlike speculative trading where insiders dump on retail, Regarded Games is a closed-loop economic simulation. It stages a quarterly battle between two opposing ideologies: **Capitalism** and **Socialism**.

### The Objective

Two factions compete to shift the game's economic state—measured by the [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient)—towards their goal.

*   **The Capitalist Goal:** Concentrate wealth. Push the [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient) towards **1.0**.
*   **The Socialist Goal:** Redistribute wealth. Push the [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient) towards **0.0**.

The first side to move the economy **25%** toward their target wins the season and dictates how the Prize Pool is distributed.

| **$FIM** | Fake Internet Money | **Gameplay.** The seasonal token you trade inside the simulation to change the [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient). |

## 3. Game Mechanics

Each season lasts for one quarter (3 months) and follows a strict three-phase cycle.

### Phase 1: Auction
**Objective: Capital Formation & Entry**

Players enter the game by minting the seasonal token, **$FIM**, using stablecoin collateral (USDC).

*   **The Collateral Rule:** To prevent [Sybil attacks](#sybil-attack), you must hold **$REG** tokens equal to **10%** of the value of $FIM you wish to buy. This ensures everyone has skin in the game.
*   **The Prize Pool:** All USDC collected forms the Prize Pool, which is immediately deployed into DeFi protocols to generate yield.

### Phase 2: Trading
**Objective: Move the [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient)**

For the duration of the season, players trade $FIM on the internal **Peer-to-Peer Exchange**.

*   **Transparent Order Book:** You can see exactly *who* is buying and selling.
*   **Gini-Aware Execution:** Every trade impacts the [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient).
    *   Selling to a **Whale** $\to$ Increases Inequality (Capitalist Move).
    *   Selling to a **Plebeian** $\to$ Reduces Inequality (Socialist Move).
*   **Strategy:** You must choose your counterparties carefully to help your faction win.

### Phase 3: Victory and Payouts
The game ends when one faction shifts the [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient) by **25%** from its starting point.

#### Scenario A: Capitalist Victory (The Oligarchy)
*   **Condition:** The [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient) increases significantly.
*   **The Payout:** Winner-Take-All. The **Oligarchy** (the smallest group of whales holding >50% of the supply) splits the entire Prize Pool.
*   **The Losers:** Everyone else gets $0.

#### Scenario B: Socialist Victory (The Solidarity Fund)
*   **Condition:** The [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient) decreases significantly.
*   **The Payout:** Redistribution.
    *   **The Cap:** The winnings of the richest players are capped.
    *   **The Fund:** The surplus is seized and distributed to *all* players based on their **Net Contribution**.
*   **The Philosophy:** Those who sacrificed capital to hold the line are rewarded.

## 5. Governance

Regarded Games utilizes a **Hybrid Governance Model** to ensure decisions are secure, legal, and accessible.

### 1. The Legal Wrapper
The DAO is wrapped in a **US-based DAO LLC**. This provides a liability shield for contributors while contractually binding the entity to execute the will of the token holders.

### 2. Snapshot (The Voice)
Voting is conducted on **Snapshot**, allowing for **gasless** participation.
*   **Voting Power:** Determined by your held + staked $REG.
*   **Scope:** Holders vote on Game Rules (adjusting the Multiplier), Treasury Spending, and Election of Council members.

### 3. The Execution Council (The Hands)
A Gnosis Safe Multi-Sig wallet acts as the operational arm of the DAO.
*   **Role:** The Council initiates the on-chain transactions approved by Snapshot votes.
*   **Safety:** The Council has a non-discretionary mandate. They *must* execute passed votes unless a critical security threat is identified.



## 6. Security

### Security Strategy
We employ a **Defense-in-Depth** approach:
1.  **Competitive Audit:** Before launch, the codebase undergoes a competitive audit via platforms like Code4rena to identify vulnerabilities via crowdsourced incentives.
2.  **Bug Bounty:** A permanent bounty program on Immunefi rewards white-hat hackers for responsible disclosure.
3.  **Emergency Pause:** The Execution Council holds the power to pause the game contracts in the event of a zero-day exploit, pending a DAO vote to resume.



## 7. Glossary

### Gini Coefficient
A statistical measure of wealth distribution used to gauge economic inequality. The score ranges from **0.0 (Perfect Equality)** to **1.0 (Absolute Concentration)**. The Regarded Games Gini Coefficient is calculated on-chain and is the single arbiter of the game's state.

### Sybil Attack
The act of one malicious entity creating and controlling multiple pseudo-anonymous identities (wallets) in order to gain a disproportionate amount of influence, votes, or rewards from a system.

### Sybil Resistance
Mechanisms designed to make a [Sybil attack](#sybil-attack) economically irrational or technically impossible. In Regarded Games, the **GINI Collateral Rule** is the primary Sybil Resistance mechanism, enforcing a high financial cost on any actor attempting to manipulate the system with multiple wallets.

### Net Contribution
A core metric calculated as a player's total money spent (MoneyIn) minus their total money sold for (MoneyOut). This metric is **Sybil-resistant** and is used to determine proportional rewards for the winning Socialist faction, ensuring that players who risked the most for the collective cause are rewarded.
