# Ritardo Games: An Introduction

Ritardo Games is a perfect-information strategy game where collective action battles economic power for real-money stakes. It is a live, on-chain economic simulation designed to confront the systemic flaws of modern finance.

The platform stages a quarterly battle between two opposing economic philosophies: Capitalism and Socialism. Your objective is to use strategic trading to drive the economy toward your chosen ideology's goal.

## The Two Factions

The state of the economic battle is tracked by the [Gini Coefficient](#gini-coefficient).

### The Objective

The first side to achieve 25% of the total possible progress toward their goal wins the season and enforces its philosophical payout structure on the entire Prize Pool.

*   The Capitalist Goal: Push the Gini Coefficient towards 1.0 (Concentration of wealth).
*   The Socialist Goal: Push the Gini Coefficient towards 0.0 (Redistribution of wealth).

## The Genesis Program (Meritocratic Distribution)

The Ritardo Games DAO is launching with a Meritocratic Distribution. Your share of the initial supply is determined by your **Contribution Score**.

### Overview & Scoring

The scoring formula rewards value-add actions and strategic contribution.

### Quest 1: The Citizen (Community)
Secure your identity and prove your strategic value in the community.
*   **Action:** Register on the platform.
*   **Bonus:** Earn a 5x Multiplier by being an active, constructive voice in our community forums.

### Quest 2: The Recruiter (Growth)
Incentivize organic ecosystem growth.
*   **Action:** Share your unique referral code.
*   **Anti-Sybil Mechanism:** Points are only awarded for referees who meet a Proof-of-Engagement threshold, ensuring you are rewarded for quality, active players, not low-effort bots.

### Quest 3: The Strategist (Testnet)
Prove your mastery of the game theory in the competitive preseason.
*   **Action:** Participate in the Testnet simulation.
*   **Weighting:** The largest score bonus is awarded to the members of the Winning Faction—a direct reward for achieving collective action and strategic coordination.

### Quest 4: The Guardian (Security)
Help secure the foundational integrity of the protocol.
*   **Action:** Discover and responsibly report technical bugs or economic exploits.
*   **Reward:** Earn a massive bonus based on the severity of the vulnerability reported.

## Game Mechanics

### The Game Loop

The entire simulation follows a structured, three-phase cycle each quarter.

### Phase 1: The Auction (1 Month)
**Goal:** Capital Formation & Accountability.

*   **Entry:** Players mint the seasonal token, $FIM, using USDC. All USDC forms the Prize Pool.
*   **Collateral Requirement:** Participation is gated. To ensure Sybil Resistance, a player must hold $RTD$ equal to 10% of their $FIM$ buy, aligning their long-term interests with the protocol's stability.
*   **Accountability:** Each player's capital is recorded as Net Contribution to enable the "Proof of Sacrifice" metric for the Socialist payout.

### Phase 2: The Simulation (Trading)
**Goal:** Strategic Gini Manipulation.

*   **The Marketplace:** Trading occurs on our internal Peer-to-Peer Exchange.
*   **Gini-Aware Trading:** Every transaction is a strategic choice. The open order book allows players to analyze a counterparty's holdings and execute a trade based on its calculated impact on the global Gini Coefficient.

### Phase 3: Victory & Payouts
The distribution of the Prize Pool is the financial fulfillment of the winning ideology.

### Scenario A: Capitalist Victory (The Oligarchy)
*   **The Payout:** Winner-Take-All. The smallest group of players controlling over 50% of the $FIM$ supply (the Oligarchy) splits the entire Prize Pool. All other players receive zero.

### Scenario B: Socialist Victory (The Solidarity Fund)
*   **The Payout:** Redistribution. Winnings for the top 50% are capped. The resulting surplus is collected into the Solidarity Fund and redistributed to all players based on their Net Contribution (Proof of Sacrifice).

### Revenue & Value Accrual

The DAO utilizes a Dynamic Revenue Allocation model.

*   **Source:** USDC profits generated by the Prize Pool's deployment into secure DeFi protocols.
*   **Value Creation:** $RTD$ holders vote on how to deploy this revenue:
    1.  **Deflation:** Use profits to execute buybacks and burn $RTD$.
    2.  **Stability:** Use profits to boost the token's market liquidity.
    3.  **Growth:** Reinvest profits to increase the next season's Prize Pool (Jackpot Multiplier).

### Supply Distribution (The 75% Community Standard)

## Governance

### The DAO & Ownership

Ritardo Games is structured as a DAO wrapped in a US-based DAO LLC.

*   **Credible Neutrality:** The rules are code, immutable by the founding team, and governed by the community.
*   **Your Power:** Ownership of $RTD$ grants you the power to vote on game balance, risk parameters, and treasury allocation.

### The Coordination Hub

Effective collective action requires infrastructure. The **Ritardo Forum** serves as the **Coordination Hub** for the community, providing:

*   **Strategic Planning:** Dedicated channels for Capitalist and Socialist factions to organize market moves.
*   **Governance Debate:** A formal space for long-form discussion and analysis before proposals go to a final vote.

### The Voting System

*   **Snapshot:** All voting is conducted on Snapshot, ensuring gasless (free) participation for all $RTD$ holders.
*   **The Execution Council:** A Gnosis Safe Multi-Sig acts as the executive arm, initiating the on-chain transactions approved by the DAO's votes.

## Security

### Audits & Risks

The foundation of the project relies on a multi-layered Defense-in-Depth strategy.

*   **Pre-Launch Security:** The code undergoes a **Competitive Audit** to leverage adversarial crowdsourcing for vulnerability identification.
*   **Post-Launch Security:** A permanent **Bug Bounty Program** provides continuous scrutiny.
*   **Mitigation:** The DAO's ability to **Emergency Pause** the game and patch rules for future seasons ensures anti-fragility against both contract and economic exploits.


## Glossary
### Gini Coefficient

It is an objective metric that measures the inequality of wealth distribution. Think of it as the ultimate scoreboard for the battle of ideas:
*   0.0 = Perfect Equality.
*   1.0 = Absolute Concentration.
