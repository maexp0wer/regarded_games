# Intro

**Ritardo Games** is a perfect-information strategy game where **Capital** battles **Coordination** for real-money stakes.

Unlike speculative trading where insiders dump on retail, Ritardo Games is a closed-loop economic simulation. It stages a quarterly battle between two opposing ideologies: **Capitalism** and **Socialism**. Everything is on-chain, rules are transparent, and victory depends entirely on collective strategy.

## The Objective

Two factions compete to shift the game's economic state—measured by the **Gini Coefficient**—towards their goal.

*   **The Capitalist Goal:** Concentrate wealth. Push the Gini Coefficient towards **1.0**.
*   **The Socialist Goal:** Redistribute wealth. Push the Gini Coefficient towards **0.0**.

The first side to move the economy **25%** toward their target wins the season and dictates how the Prize Pool is distributed.

## The Core Assets

The ecosystem revolves around a dual-token structure:


| Asset | Name | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $RTD | The Ritardo | Governance & Access. You must stake $RTD to play the game and vote on the rules. |
| $FIM | Fake Internet Money | Gameplay. The seasonal token you trade inside the simulation to change the Gini Coefficient. |
